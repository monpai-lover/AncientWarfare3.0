# Xiaization Naming And Name Integration Design

## Goal

Separate culture-level personal naming from kingdom-level institutional
Xiaization, convert living members of an integrated culture to stable Xia names,
and repair the surname/clan-name integration policy so its effects are complete,
consistent, and recoverable.

## Authoritative Gates

Personal naming and kingdom institutions use different authorities:

- A person's culture having `aw_xia_integrated` or
  `aw_xia_fully_integrated` selects the Xia personal naming profile.
- A kingdom reaching Xiaization level 5 selects Xia technology, policy, and
  court profiles.
- Completing `aw_policy_name_integration` marks that kingdom's surname/clan-name
  integration complete.

Culture integration does not automatically complete surname integration. A
shared integrated culture may give people in several kingdoms Xia names, but it
must not switch a non-level-5 kingdom's technology, policy tree, court system,
or marriage/history gates.

## Fully Xiaized Kingdom Transition

Add a versioned, phased, replayable kingdom transition whose authority is
`XiaizationService.GetLevel(kingdom) == 5`, not a shared culture trait. It:

1. selects the Xia technology, policy, and court profiles while retaining
   common nodes and shared resources;
2. archives Western-only nodes and removes invalid current, completed, locked,
   and queued decisions;
3. removes obsolete Western offices with a bounded cleanup;
4. immediately recalculates court offices, factions, and the dominant school;
5. records phase/version progress so a load interruption can resume safely.

The transition never changes war type, diplomatic participants, or unrelated
kingdom identity. A culture trait write failure cannot mark the kingdom
transition complete. Multiple kingdoms sharing a culture are evaluated by
their own level and therefore cannot switch one another's institutions.

## Integrated-Culture Personal Naming

The naming-profile resolver treats both integrated culture traits as Xia for
actors and births. Future births use the full Xia naming path even when the
parents are ordinary, have no office, or lack an existing lineage record.

Existing living actors are handled by a culture-scoped, actor-ID-ordered,
bounded migration. Before each write it rechecks that the actor still belongs
to the integrated culture. Actors who moved culture, vanished, or died are
skipped. Actors arriving later are picked up by the normal admission/repair
boundary.

Actors with an existing Western or Orc branch receive a Xia child branch. Only
living members move to it; dead archives stay on the historical branch. An
actor with no lineage receives a stable Xia naming identity but does not cause
bulk creation of a fictional family tree.

Player-custom names and authored historical figures, including protected
historical masters, retain their displayed name. Their cultural/profile state
may be recorded without overwriting the protected text.

## Western Stem Conversion

Western-to-Xia conversion resolves the family surname once per old branch and
persists the mapping. It recognizes common prefixes such as `德`, `冯`, `范`,
and `迪` followed by optional spaces or `·`/`・`. After removing the prefix and
separator, it takes the first CJK character from the core surname:

`德·贾阿拉 -> 贾`

If no valid CJK character exists, a deterministic Xia surname fallback is used.
The clan/shi name is generated by a separate stable rule and must differ from
the surname when possible. Two old branches that both resolve to `贾` retain
different lineage and shi IDs; identical visible surnames never merge unrelated
genealogies.

The database branch transaction commits before live actors are rebound. An
existing Xia child branch is reused, making repeated migration idempotent.

## Surname/Clan-Name Integration Repair

The kingdom's `aw_name_integrated` value is the authoritative statement that
the policy is complete. The actor value with the same key means only that this
actor's integrated name has been materialized and archived. They remain
separate because they live on different data owners.

Replace the current one-shot, per-actor random pass with a versioned,
branch-aware materialization service:

1. policy completion writes the kingdom authority and starts/resumes the job;
2. load restoration detects a completed policy or persisted kingdom state and
   repairs missing runtime flags instead of assuming the old effect finished;
3. members of one family/shi branch receive one stable clan name, never one
   random value per actor;
4. living Xia-profile actors without a branch receive a stable personal
   clan-name projection without manufacturing a genealogy;
5. births, immigrants, and later admissions materialize the actor flag and
   archive state whenever their current kingdom is integrated;
6. display and archive projection use clan/shi plus given name after
   integration, while the old family surname no longer controls the marriage
   restriction.

Completing cultural integration alone does not set the kingdom flag, relax the
same-surname marriage rule, or satisfy the historical-figure arrival gate.
Those behaviors remain tied to the independent policy.

## Persistence and Scheduling

Both kingdom and culture migrations store schema/version, phase, cursor, and
failure information. Candidate ordering is stable. Work is frame-budgeted and
never performs an unbounded global actor scan on a hot path.

The publish boundary follows database success. A save/load, duplicate policy
effect, multiplayer replay, or reconnect may run a phase again with zero
additional mutation. Multiplayer hosts perform authoritative branch creation
and project outcomes; clients consume persisted/replicated identity rather than
choosing random names.

## Failure Handling

Malformed old names use deterministic fallback rather than aborting the whole
job. Database failure rolls back the branch or integration batch and leaves live
actors unchanged. Each candidate is revalidated for life, culture, kingdom,
profile, protected-name status, and existing Xia identity immediately before
commit and publish.

Diagnostics identify migration version, phase, cursor, kingdom/culture/actor or
branch ID, decision, and retry reason. They are sampled and bounded.

## Verification

Tests must cover:

- `Integrated=true, Fully=false` selecting Xia actor naming while the kingdom
  retains its Western policy/court/technology profile;
- level 5 switching only that kingdom's institutional profiles even when its
  culture is shared;
- integrated-culture ordinary births, old-save residents, immigrants, culture
  transfers, and actors without lineage receiving stable Xia names;
- protected custom and historical names plus unchanged dead archives;
- all supported Western prefixes and separators, first-CJK extraction,
  deterministic fallback, branch reuse, and same-visible-surname branch
  separation;
- a completed integration policy repairing missing kingdom/actor flags on
  load, consistent clan name within a branch, newborn and immigrant
  materialization, and no random family splits;
- continued separation of culture naming, level-5 institutional transition,
  surname-integration marriage relaxation, and historical-figure gate;
- database rollback, bounded cursor resume, multiplayer host authority,
  archive/live consistency, and repeat-run idempotence;
- full naming, lineage, policy, court, migration, archive, source-guard, and
  rules suites plus the net48 mod build.
